Privacy Policy

What we collect

When you place an order we collect your name, phone number, delivery address and the items you ordered. We use this only to deliver your order and to contact you about it.

How we use it

  • To confirm, pack and deliver your order.
  • To call or message you about your order.
  • To improve our website and our advertising.

Cookies and analytics

We use cookies, the Meta (Facebook) Pixel and Google Analytics to see which pages are visited and which products are popular, and to show our ads to people who may like them. These tools receive information such as the pages you view, the products you add to your cart and your order value.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with our delivery partners to deliver your order, and with the analytics and advertising services above.

Your choices

You can block cookies in your browser settings. You can also ask us to delete your order details.

Contact

For any question about your information, use the phone, WhatsApp or email shown at the bottom of this page.
