Return & Exchange Policy

Time limit

Tell us within 3 days of receiving your order if you want to exchange or return a product.

What we accept

  • The size does not fit.
  • The product is damaged or has a defect.
  • You received a different product from what you ordered.

Conditions

  • The product must be unused, unwashed and have its tags attached.
  • Please keep the original packaging.

Who pays for delivery

If the product is damaged, defective or not what you ordered, we pay for the return delivery. For size exchanges and other returns, the customer pays the delivery charge.

How to return

Contact us using the phone, WhatsApp or email shown at the bottom of this page. Send your order details and a photo of the product. We will tell you the next step.

Refunds

Once we receive and check the product, we refund the product price or send an exchange.
